# Event Surprise V2 Loop Plan

The loop follows ten bounded cycles. Every cycle creates an implementation commit and a separate checkpoint commit, runs targeted and full applicable tests, and stops on a critical hold.

1. Recovery and pre-registration.
2. Human-gold benchmark preparation.
3. Model ladder and Lock A.
4. Frozen corpus validation or resumable inference.
5. Outcome-blind clustering and feature panel.
6. Point-in-time outcome panel and Lock B.
7. Locked out-of-sample evaluation.
8. Conditional economic interpretation.
9. Public-safe evidence product and real-human-review packet.
10. Paper, reproducibility, career package, sanitized Obsidian, and sanitized Graphify.

The existing v1 negative result is a baseline, not a tunable development target. The new loop will stop at Cycle 6 if a genuinely untouched outcome panel cannot be established.
